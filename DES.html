5. Median

The data are already arranged in ascending order:

Since there are 6 observations, the median is the average of the 3rd and 4th observations.

Median = (3rd value + 4th value) / 2
Median = (7 + 9) / 2
Median = 16 / 2 = 8
Answer: Median = 8

10. Sample Variance

Sample Variance: s² = Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1)
s² = 171.3333 / (6 − 1)
s² = 171.3333 / 5
s² = 34.2667
Answer: Sample Variance = 34.2667

11. Sample Standard Deviation

s = √[Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1)]
s = √34.2667
s = 5.8538
Answer: Standard Deviation = 5.8538

12. Standard Error

SE = s / √n
SE = 5.8538 / √6
√6 ≈ 2.4495
SE = 5.8538 / 2.4495
SE ≈ 2.3898
Answer: Standard Error = 2.3898

13. Skewness

We use the sample skewness formula, which is the formula corresponding to Excel's SKEW.

Skewness = [n / ((n − 1)(n − 2))] × [Σ(x − x̄)³ / s³]

From the calculation table:

Σ(x − x̄)³ = 604.1111

We already calculated:

s = 5.8538

Therefore:

s³ ≈ 200.54

Substitute into the formula:

Skewness = [6 / ((6 − 1)(6 − 2))] × [604.1111 / 200.54]
= (6 / 20) × 3.012
= 0.3 × 3.012
≈ 0.9040
Answer: Skewness = 0.9040
Interpretation: Since skewness is positive (0.9040), the distribution is positively skewed / right-skewed. The larger value 19 pulls the distribution toward the right.

14. Kurtosis

We use the sample excess kurtosis formula, corresponding to Excel's KURT function.

Kurtosis = [n(n + 1) / ((n − 1)(n − 2)(n − 3))] × [Σ(x − x̄)⁴ / s⁴] − [3(n − 1)² / ((n − 2)(n − 3))]

From the table:

Σ(x − x̄)⁴ = 10899.8889

We have:

s = 5.8538
s⁴ ≈ 1173.13

Substitute the values:

Kurtosis = [6(7) / (5 × 4 × 3)] × [10899.8889 / 1173.13] − [3(5²) / (4 × 3)]
= (42 / 60) × 9.291 − 75 / 12
= 0.7 × 9.291 − 6.25
≈ 6.5037 − 6.25
≈ 0.2503
Answer: Kurtosis = 0.2503
Interpretation: The excess kurtosis is positive (0.2503), so the data are slightly leptokurtic compared with a normal distribution.
